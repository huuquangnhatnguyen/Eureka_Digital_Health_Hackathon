## The Problem

SMART@Home (Self-Management Assistance for Recommended Treatment at Home) is a Cincinnati Children's platform that helps patients manage chronic and complex conditions (asthma, IBD, migraine, and more) at home. It already collects rich patient data, but clinicians see it as **raw table rows**, which don't work in a short visit.

A purpose-built view should help when:

- **Time is short.** The clinician needs "what changed and what matters now" right away.
- **Specialties differ.** Different specialists need different levels of detail from the same data.
- **Trends matter.** The team needs to compare a patient's status and responses over time, which is hard today.

## Scope

We are redesigning **one view**: what a clinician sees, compares, and acts on when they open a patient's data. We are not redesigning the whole platform.

Starting points: the existing **Patients**, **Trackers**, and **Studies** tabs and the per-patient logs.

## Guiding Questions

- What does a clinician need to see in the **first 30 seconds** of opening a patient's record?
- Which data points **drive treatment decisions**, and which are noise?
- How do needs **differ across specialties** viewing the same data?
- How should the view support **verifying adherence, spotting trends, flagging anomalies, and guiding treatment**?

## Background: SMART@Home Today

- **Built on behavioral science.** Over 20 years of adherence research (positive reinforcement, gamification, reminders).
- **Co-created.** Patients and clinicians co-design the trackers and care logic.
- **No-code.** Clinicians configure condition-specific trackers in hours instead of months.
- **Rapid adaptation.** New trackers deploy in minutes and update from patient feedback.
- **One unified app.** Many conditions in one app, with data flowing into Epic.
